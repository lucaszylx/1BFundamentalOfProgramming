PERFORMANCE TASK SHEET
PIZZA
Performance Objective: Given the tools, materials, and equipment you must be able to perform the task in preparing Pizza, following the standard operating procedure.
Supplies/Materials: PPE (hairnet, apron, towels, gloves)
Ingredients:
Crust:
2 tbsp sugar
1 tsp instant yeast
½ cup warm water
2 tbsp oil
1 ½ cup APF
Pinch of salt 		Toppings:
Pizza sauce
Your choice of toppings
Equipment: Mixing bowl, rubber scraper, sifter, baking sheet, dough cutter, weighing scale, spatula, cling wrap, oven.
Steps/Procedure:
  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the sugar, yeast, warm water, and oil. Let the yeast bloom for 5–10 minutes.
  2. Add the all-purpose flour and salt, then mix to form a dough.
  3. Knead the dough for 30 minutes, or until it becomes elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with cling wrap or a cloth, and let it ferment for 1 hour, or until it doubles in size.
  5. Punch down the dough, transfer it to a greased pan, and shape it as desired. Prick the dough with a fork.
  6. Spread the pizza sauce evenly and add your preferred toppings.
  7. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes, or until the crust turns golden brown.
Assessment Method: Demonstration and Direct Observation.
